01 / The feeling

The best photos are never really about posing.

They are the ears that lift at a familiar sound, the sudden sprint across the grass, and the way your dog leans into you when the day is quiet.

I photograph dogs around Calgary — by the river, in golden parks, and on the trails they already love — so you can keep the personality you know by heart.

No pressure. No perfect behaviour required. Just your dog, being wonderfully themselves.
