Good to know

Questions, answered simply.

The session should feel easy from the first message. Here is everything most dog parents want to know before we begin.

What is included in the $299 session?
The Calgary Dog Story Session is $299 CAD + GST. It includes up to 60 relaxed minutes outdoors, your dog and owner/family together, up to two dogs from one household, and 10 hand-edited high-resolution images. Your private online gallery will arrive within seven days.
My dog will not sit still — or is very shy. Can we still do this?
Of course. Perfect behaviour is not the goal. We will work at your dog’s pace and capture the natural parts of who they are: the curious look, the sudden run, the quiet pause, and all the little expressions you know best.
Can I be in the photographs too?
Yes. Your dog and their people belong in this story together. Owners and close family are always welcome to step into a few frames.
Where will we take the photos?
We will choose a Calgary outdoor location that feels right for your dog. Locations in Calgary’s north and downtown are included. If your ideal spot is farther south or outside the city, I will confirm any small travel fee with you before you book.
What happens if the weather is not right?
We will reschedule. Your dog’s comfort and a good experience matter more than forcing a session through wind, rain, extreme cold, or unsafe conditions.
When will I receive my photographs?
Your private online gallery will be ready within seven days. Every final image is carefully hand-edited to feel warm, detailed and true to your dog — with expressive eyes, natural colour and fur you can almost feel.
Can I choose the editing style?
Yes. You can choose the finish that feels most like your dog: Natural & Timeless for clean, true-to-life colour, or Warm & Golden for soft warmth with a sunlit, heartfelt feel.
Can I order framed prints, wall art or an album?
Yes. After your gallery arrives, you can choose optional framed prints, wall art and heirloom albums for the images you want to live with every day.
How do I book?
Send an email with your dog’s name and a little about their personality, or message Max directly on WhatsApp. We will find a location and time that feels easy for you both.
