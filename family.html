Meet the family

Before I photograph your dog, I am a pet parent too.

Our home is shared with two dogs, two cats, and the lasting love of a Chow Chow we had to say goodbye to. This is where the heart behind Dog Stories by Max begins.

Why it matters

I know the little things are never little.

The ears that rise when you say one word. The place they wait for you. The expression that only their person recognizes. I photograph dogs with that kind of love in mind.

Buddy

From little brother to big brother.

Buddy has always loved being around people and other dogs. He is the kind of dog who wants to be part of every hello, every walk and every little moment of fun.

When ChuChu was still with us, Buddy was the little brother: always looking for a game, a reaction or simply a moment close to him. When dinner was being prepared, Buddy would sit patiently in the kitchen — although he was never against checking whether ChuChu might share a little extra.

Buddy and ChuChu arrived home on the same day and grew up side by side. After ChuChu passed away, Buddy would smell the harness his brother used to wear and cry. Losing the companion he had known every day of his life brought him deep separation anxiety.

That is why, while Buddy was still only two, we brought Charlie into our family. In a short time, Buddy went from being the little brother to becoming a big brother — and found companionship again.
